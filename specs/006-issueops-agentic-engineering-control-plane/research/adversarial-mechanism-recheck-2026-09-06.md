### Reuse vocabulary and admission boundary

For new decisions use exactly ADAPT, BEHAVIOR_ORACLE, SPEC_ORACLE, SECURITY_ORACLE, TEST_ORACLE, CLEAN_ROOM_REIMPLEMENT, BOUNDED_SOURCE_CANDIDATE, DEPENDENCY_CANDIDATE or REJECT. Evidence labels are PRODUCT_CLAIM, SOURCE_CODE_FACT, DOCUMENTATION_FACT, INFERENCE and RECOMMENDATION. Historical labels such as TEST_QUARRY/UX_ORACLE/FAILURE_ORACLE mean TEST_ORACLE/BEHAVIOR_ORACLE/TEST_ORACLE respectively; SOURCE_CANDIDATE means BOUNDED_SOURCE_CANDIDATE, INTERCHANGE_STANDARD means SPEC_ORACLE, and hosted-reviewer eligibility is a separate qualification attribute rather than source reuse classification. Historical source notes are not retroactive admissions.

RECOMMENDATION: preserve WePLD-owned schemas and reuse narrowly qualified adapters/mechanisms. Connector frameworks bring package/update/supply-chain burden; distributed queue products bring operational services; browser/desktop stacks bring platform/permission/containment variance. Their value must exceed that burden at the owning source gate. RESEARCH != SOURCE_ADMISSION and DEPENDENCY_CANDIDATE != DEPENDENCY_ADMITTED. No runtime, dependency, source registry or canonical governance file is changed by this study.
